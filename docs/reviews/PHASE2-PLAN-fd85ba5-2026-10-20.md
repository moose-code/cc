## 4. Contracts

All three contracts bind to the existing Core and read authority from `ICoreSale(core).owner()`, as ControllerV1 does. No proxy, no delegatecall, no `tx.origin`, no arbitrary call facility. Vocabulary "balance", plain technical comments, no em or en dashes, a `// promise:` line on every kept test.

### 4.1 `src/adapters/Phase2BalanceTreasury.sol`

Implements `IBalanceModule` for the deployed Core. Immutables: `core`, `ccToken` (checked equal to `Core.COIN()`), `balanceToken`, `statements` (checked equal to `balanceToken.statements()` and `Mainnet.STATEMENTS`), `unitPerPoint` 1e14 checked against `SCORE_TO_TOKEN` and 18 decimals.

Storage: `claimReserve` (C), `owed` and `totalOwed` (D) carried over from `StatementBalanceModule`, `minimumWorkingBalance` (F), `conversionToClaimBps` (300 at deployment, inactive until conversion is enabled), `redemptionFeeBps` (0), `conversionEnabled`, `workingPaymentsEnabled`, `ccRedemptionEnabled` (all false), `redemptionPolicy` (the V1 policy address), `configEpoch`, `migrated`, `successor`.

Views:

- `custody()` = `balanceOf(this)`; `working()` = `custody - C - D` floored at zero; `freeWorking()` = `working - F` floored at zero; `solvent()` = `custody >= C + D`.
- `conversionReady()` = conversion enabled, selected module of the bound Core, Core not retired, not migrated, and the upstream token's withdrawal preconditions from section 5.
- `transfersReady()` = the upstream token's public transfer gate from section 5.
- `available()` = `freeWorking()` only when working payments are enabled, selected, not retired, not migrated, `transfersReady()` and `solvent()`; else zero; never reverts; stays inside the Core's 2,000,000 gas read bound (five external reads).
- `quoteRedemption(ccIn)` returns `(balanceOut, claimReserve, ccSupply, configEpoch, executable)`, where `executable` is false whenever `redeemCC` would revert for reasons other than the user's own minimum and deadline.

Core only, as today: `redeem(statementId)` per spec section 5 steps 1 to 8, with the existing full rating receipt requirement (`out >= rating * unitPerPoint`), the contribution `a = floor(R * conversionToClaimBps / 10000)` added to `C`, all `R` kept in custody, and the event `StatementConverted(sid, received, allocated, contributionBps, claimReserve)`. `pay(to, amount)` unchanged in behavior from `StatementBalanceModule` (gas kept back for the accrual, shortfall recorded as debt), bounded by `available()`. `claim(to)` (the spec's `claimOwed`) pays recorded debt only and works while every switch is off and after migration.

Public: `redeemCC(ccIn, recipient, minBalanceOut, deadline, expectedConfigEpoch)`:

1. Requires enabled, selected, not retired, not migrated, `transfersReady()`, `deadline >= now`, `expectedConfigEpoch == configEpoch`, `recipient` not zero and not this contract, `0 < ccIn <= totalSupply`.
2. Snapshots `S = totalSupply()` and `C`. Computes `cap = mulDiv(ccIn, C, S)` (full precision).
3. Static calls the policy with `(ccIn, C, S, redemptionFeeBps)` under a fixed gas bound and 32 byte return; requires success and `0 < out <= cap`. The treasury's `cap` is the custody rule; the policy can only lower it.
4. Requires `out >= minBalanceOut`, `out <= C`, custody solvent after payment.
5. `C -= out`; `burnFrom(msg.sender, ccIn)`; requires `totalSupply()` fell by exactly `ccIn`; `safeTransfer(recipient, out)`; requires the recipient's balance rose by `out` and custody fell by `out`. Any failure reverts the whole call. No deferred user debt.
6. Emits `CCRedeemed(caller, recipient, ccIn, out, claimReserveAfter, supplyAfter, configEpoch)`.

Owner: `setSwitches(conversion, workingPayments, ccRedemption)`, `setConversionToClaimBps`, `setRedemptionFeeBps`, `setMinimumWorkingBalance`, `setRedemptionPolicy` (requires code, a successful static probe with the treasury's own bindings, and turns `ccRedemptionEnabled` off), `fundClaims(amount)` (measured `transferFrom` receipt, refused at zero CC supply), `allocateToClaims(amount)` (reclassify free working funds, no transfer), `returnClaimsToWorking(amount)` (requires `ccRedemptionEnabled == false`, at most `C`), `withdrawWorking(to, amount)` (at most `freeWorking()`), `rescueToken(token, to, amount)` (refuses CC, BALANCE, Credits and Statements), `migrateTo(successor)` and `receiveMigration(claimAmount)` per section 4.4. Every setter emits an event. `configEpoch` increments on policy change, fee change, `returnClaimsToWorking`, `migrateTo` and `receiveMigration`; not on conversions, redemptions, `allocateToClaims` or `fundClaims` (those only raise the quote under the same terms).

Guard: one storage flag over `redeem`, `pay`, `claim`, `redeemCC`, `fundClaims`, `allocateToClaims`, `returnClaimsToWorking`, `withdrawWorking`, `migrateTo`, `receiveMigration` and `rescueToken`. Set and cleared per call, so the Core's two sequential `pay` calls pass. The accrual path of `pay` must fit the Core's 600,000 gas artist bound with the guard; the focused test measures it.

Unsolicited transfers raise `working()` by construction, never `C`.

### 4.2 `src/adapters/ProRataRedemptionPolicy.sol`

Pure, stateless, holds nothing, approved for nothing. `quote(ccIn, claimReserve, ccSupply, feeBps) -> uint256`: `gross = mulDiv(ccIn, claimReserve, ccSupply)`, `out = gross * (10000 - feeBps) / 10000`; zero inputs and `ccIn > ccSupply` revert. Any retained fee stays in `C` because the treasury subtracts only `out`. Exposes `VERSION = "pro-rata-v1"` and `isRedemptionPolicy() == true` for the treasury's probe.

### 4.3 `src/ControllerV2.sol`

Starts from ControllerV1 and changes four things; everything else, including `refreshReference`, the credit sale window, `nextPage`, `pastWindow`, `valuePerPoint`, `balanceClampFor`, `floorMultBps`, `setBalancePoolKey`, `setQuote`, `setCredit` and the loop closure checks, is carried over unchanged.

1. **Price guard (issue 113).** In `_protected`: read the live spot and live liquidity; require live liquidity and both samples at least `minLiquidity`; `pBuy = min(prior sample, live spot, ref + tol)`, `pSell = max(prior sample, live spot, ref - tol)`. Both the views (`project` true) and the doors read the live spot in the same block, so view and door parity holds. A malformed live read fails closed as today.
2. **Statement sale policy.** Replace the `Sale` struct and anchor machinery with `statementSalesEnabled` (false at deployment), `statementSaleMultiplierBps` (uint32, 30,000), `statementRedeemDelaySeconds` (uint32, 3,600), one setter each with events. `_quote` becomes `max(cost * saleFloorBps / 10000, mulDivRoundingUp(reference, multiplier, 10000))`, with the reference exactly as V1 computes it in each mode. `buy` reverts `StatementSalesOff` while disabled; `priceOf` still answers so the Core's bounded read and the website keep a number.
3. **Redemption eligibility.** `atFloor(sid)` = statement held and `CORE.statementAge(sid) >= statementRedeemDelaySeconds`. The Core's age excludes paused time already. `ageEligibilityOn`, `decayHours`, `anchorBps`, `startCapBps` and `anchorMoveBps` are gone as settings.
4. **Legacy views.** `markupBps()` returns the multiplier clamped to `uint16.max`; `decayHours()` returns the delay in hours rounded up; `anchorBps()` returns 10,000; `startCapBps()` returns the multiplier. All are documented as legacy read only views for existing ABI consumers and are removed from the packages, indexer and web in the follow up packet. There is no shadow setting to drift.

Constructor: `(core, CreditSale, Quote, statementMultiplierBps, statementRedeemDelaySeconds)`. The deployment script reads `CreditSale` from the live V1 (`saleOn`, `window`, `spreadBps`, `startBps`) and passes a zero `Quote` so no price reads the pool until the owner sets it.

### 4.4 Migration

`migrateTo(successor)`, owner only on the source: requires all three switches off, successor code, `successor.core() == core`, `successor.ccToken() == ccToken`, `successor.balanceToken() == balanceToken`, `successor.predecessor() == this`, and the successor disabled. Moves `custody - D` (keeps `D` behind for `claim`), calls `successor.receiveMigration(C)`, sets `C = 0`, `migrated = true`, bumps the epoch, emits `ReserveMigrated`. `receiveMigration(claimAmount)`, callable only by the recorded predecessor, requires the measured balance increase at least `claimAmount`, adds `claimAmount` to its own `C` and bumps its epoch. A migrated source refuses every switch forever; later inflows to it leave through `withdrawWorking` only, which is the documented recovery path and never recreates `C`.

The successor also keeps `core()` and `balanceToken()` so an older `StatementBalanceModule` could reach it through `migrateInactiveReserve`; no such module is deployed today, so this is compatibility only.

## 7. Calibration script (read only)

`script/phase2/calibrate.py`, no signing, no state change. Inputs: pinned block, authenticated CC, hook and pool, BALANCE token and its pool, candidate reserve list and the operator's maximum allocation, test sizes (default 0.1, 0.5, 1 percent of `totalSupply`), target net margin (default 200 bps), gas and base fee assumptions, slippage stress, `minimumWorkingBalance` and the ETH processing reserve.

For each candidate `C` and size `x`: `Q` from a fork simulation of the actual hooked buy of net `x` CC (exact output, fees inside the hook, no separate haircut), `Y = quote(x, C, S, 0)`, `V` from a fork simulation of selling `Y` BALANCE on its pool, `G` the full gas estimate of buy, approve, `redeemCC` and sell. The report prints `profit = V - Q - G` and `margin = profit / (Q + G)` per row, flags unsupported sizes and thin liquidity, and prints, separately, the backing per token at each `C` on both the total and circulating denominators and the working funds left after the floor.

The recommendation line reads: smallest `C` within budget that meets the owner's backing target. The arbitrage rows are printed next to it with the sentence "an immediate arbitrage exists at size s" or "no budgeted reserve creates an immediate arbitrage; proportional claims remain useful". The script never raises a candidate above budget. A quoter that does not reproduce the hook is rejected with a message, not substituted.
