## checked, no finding

- eth leaves only through the doors or `migrate`: read every `call`, `safeTransferETH` and `settle` in Core.sol and CoreLib.sol. `_sellForEth` pays the booked read per credit through `_spend`; `buyListing` pays `value <= bid <= ethPot` plus `paid <= bid - value` and checks `balance + value >= ethBefore`; `_payCaller` is capped by bounds and the pot; `buyback` spends `owed <= budget` and pays at most `maxPay`; `claimPayee` pays `payeeOwed`; `_moveEth` zeroes `ethPot` before the send.
- the owner takes nothing beyond the accepted powers (apart from F2): read every `onlyOwner` and `_onlyOwner` path. `rescueCoin` moves coin only; `rescueNft` refuses pile credits and held statements and needs `ownerOf == core`; the settings bounds cap every keeper and caller payment; `setPayee` and `royaltyRecipient` redirect claims (accepted, SPEC 11).
- a credit is never bought above the bid: `_bid()` is `min(price state, ethPot)` read after `_book` and `_checkpoint`; `buyListing` recomputes it after the checkpoint and the same block cannot climb during the external call (`nowTs > t` gate in `_climb`).
- a statement never leaves below the hard floor or without the minimum: `sellTo` checks `cost * saleFloorBps`, `_unhold` runs before the transfer; `redeemStatement` computes `required` from the live unit before `_unhold`, measures the token delta around `redeem`, reverts `Underpaid`. `_ask` returns exactly `ref * floor / BPS` at the floor, so `atFloor` is exact.
- fees reach the pot by themselves and only fees are shared: every door books first (`_openDoor`, or `_book` in `sellTo`, `sellCreditTo`, `redeemStatement`, `buyback`, `fund`, `collectEscrow`, `setSettings`); `_income` excludes `msg.value`; `receive` admits HOOK and ESCROW only. in the hook, a push to the Core cannot fail (0 gas plus the 2,300 stipend against a sender compare), so the escrow holds Core eth only as the partial fill refund of its own buyback (`over` credited to the swap sender); an external `escrow.claim(core, 0)` turns that refund into shared income, documented in SPEC 5 and unreachable in practice (needs the buyback to hit `MIN_SQRT_PRICE + 1`).
- the payee latch: `_sniperOver` reads word 9 of `deploymentInfo` (offset word then `launchedAt` as the 9th field, checked against `DeploymentInfoV2`); the 200,000 gas cap is below 63/64 of the gas the rest of `_book` needs, so a caller cannot starve the read while the book succeeds. the latching book shares nothing (accepted 14).
- no door bricked by a counterparty: the controller reads (500k, 400k, 200k), the adapter quote (300k, 8,192 bytes) and the unit reads (200k Core and lib, 50k controller) are bounded and fail closed; a reverting payee or royalty recipient blocks its own claim; `_moveBalanceToken` logs and continues. a successor whose `fund()` and plain receive both revert stops `migrate` until `setSuccessor` is changed (owner choice).
- pots never exceed the balance: every pot increase matches measured eth (`_book`, `fund`, `sellTo`, `sellCreditTo`, `collectEscrow`), every decrease matches an outflow or a pot to pot move (`releaseRedeemBurn`, the redeem burn move). `balancePot` rises by the measured delta and falls by the transferred total.
- reentrancy: the solady guard in Core and `_enter`/`_leave` in CoreLib share slot `0x929eee149b4bd21268` (storage, not transient; checked lib/solady). reentry from Seaport payouts, `sellTargetNFT`, the hook push and the pool extension inside `unlock`, `redeem`, `fund`, the balance token transfer, Statements' mint callback (view) and Credits transfers (no callback) reaches only guarded doors. `unlockCallback` accepts the pool manager only, which calls back only the `unlock` caller. the controller's `_busy` spans the Core call and the refund; `claimRoyalty` zeroes first.
- the delegatecall library and the State layout: `State` at slot 0 in both; `_toLib` forwards calldata under matching selectors (address, uint256 and `uint256[]` signatures are identical in library and contract form); `setSettings` rebuilds the call under the library's struct selector; non view library functions carry the call guard, so a direct call cannot write library storage.
- the CREATE2 deploy: Core init code commits to the owner and config, so a stranger's pre deploy is the same contract and the deploy skips it; the controller and Seaport adapter commit to the Core; before `setController` the controller reads fail closed (staticcall to no code returns 0 bytes, `ok` false); the coin address depends on the owner as launcher and the factory is owner only. the deploy steps measure each transaction against the cap.
- the pause arithmetic: `pausedNow() - pauseMark` never exceeds `block.timestamp - composedAt`; a repeated `setPaused` returns early; uint40 holds every timestamp.
- `pointRate`, `valueOf` and the pool math: `mulDiv(floorRate, points, refScore)`; wei per base unit is `(Q96 / sqrtP)^2` for native `currency0`, with both quotients checked for 256 bits; the pool term can only raise a price (accepted 11). `ref * n` in `_ask` overflows only with `ref` above about 1.6e68 wei, out of reach at `MIN_SQRT_PRICE` with any unit below about 1e21.
- the royalty: `buy` and `buyCredits` take price plus royalty, forward the price, add the royalty after the Core call, refund last; the anchor and floors read the price only.
- the balance side gates and the live unit read: `redeemOn`, `balanceBidOn`, module set, `retired` refused in the bid, unit `0 < u <= uint128.max` in Core and lib, `refScore` nonzero; the balance cycle loses at most its margin because `redeemSlippageBps <= balanceBidMarginBps` is a bound.

## disagreements with the prior review (REVIEW-security-b88940c.md)

1. "no blocker, no high, no medium" and "the owner functions move assets only to the successor, rescue untracked coin and NFTs": the prior review treats every untracked credit as a donation. a credit migrated to a successor Core is untracked there by construction until `adopt`, so `rescueNft` on the successor is an owner path for engine bought credits (F2), open after `lockSuccessor`.
2. the prior review's "a credit is never bought above the bid" is correct for each purchase, but it does not check the credit sale floor against the flat bid. decision 104 replaced the flat bid backstop of decisions 88, 94 and 96(a) with `score * pointRate`, which is below the bid for any credit under the reference score, so the engine sells such credits below the price it pays for them (F1). no invariant measures an outsider's gain, which is why the suites stay green.
3. agreed: S1 (latch gap, runbook `book()`), S2 (Seaport tips, accepted 15), S3 and S4 (preflight and the pinned factory), S5 (claim redirection). no further disagreement with the "checked" list.
