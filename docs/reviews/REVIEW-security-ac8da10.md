## verified

- the eth payout is `ethRate() * score / refScore` rounded down, with the score scaled drop per credit in call order: test/PerPoint.t.sol (4), test/PerPointExact.t.sol `test_batchOfThree_eachPayoutAndRateAfterInCallOrder`, `test_rounding_payoutAndCostBasisRoundDown`.
- eth buy then sell back loses: the credit floor reads `floorRate`, which is at least the pre clamp price state and so at least every later read; both sides round down, which keeps the inequality. test/CreditSale.t.sol `test_h1_buyThenSellBackIsUnprofitable`, `test_noProfit_buyThenSellBackNeverBeatsTheBid`; PerPointExact `test_rounding_buyAtTheFloorThenSellForEthDoesNotProfit`.
- `buyListing` pays `value + paid <= bidOf(id)`: `paid` is capped at `bid - value` (Core.sol `buyListing`), the quote at the bid and the pot.
- the balance payout is at most par and the stored balance rate stays at or above the floor: test/BalanceBid.t.sol `test_payoutNeverAbovePar`, `test_dropLowersRateAndHoldsFloor`. the cap at or above the floor follows by induction: every fill sets `lastFillRate` to a price at or above the floor, and a floor raise above the stored rate resets the anchor. the paid rate is M1.
- balance custody: the Core moves the token only through one `module.pay(seller, total)` with the seller's holding checked; test/BalanceCustody.t.sol (4): underpay `BadModule`, short redemption `BadModule`, batch above available `PotTooSmall`, bid off accumulates.
- forced pushes: each cut is bounded by its bps of the payment, the payee share by `payeePpm` of income, each sent with 50,000 gas plus the selfdestruct fallback; a reverting recipient settles. test/Sale.t.sol `SaleCutsTest` (7), test/FeeShare.t.sol `test_aFailingPayeeStillReceivesThroughTheForcedPush`, `test_thePushGivesThePayeeNoReentry`.
- swap: score rule, pile node inheritance, fee burned through `burnFrom` of the caller, refusals; test/CreditSwap.t.sol (13).
- reentrancy: every door is `nonReentrant`; the library owner paths take the same guard word; every ERC721 move is `transferFrom`, so no receiver callback runs; eth and balance token sends come after the state is final.
- the two bid instances: `_climb` and `_drop` take the anchor by reference; eth paths pass `s.anchor`, balance paths pass `s.balance.anchor`; the only shared state is the settings.
- arithmetic: the balance payout is at most 8e6 * 2^128 * 1e4, under 2^256; `_toBps` returns 10,000 before `avail * BPS` can overflow.
- layout: test/CoreLayout.t.sol (3) pins `rateAtCheckpoint` 9, `payee` 21, `artist` and `payeeOn` 22, the balance instance 31 to 36; web/lib/live.ts, test/utils/Fixture.sol and the handler all read the eth anchor at 27; script/ reads no Core storage slots.
- the hard rule, outflow by outflow: `sellForEth`, `buyListing`, `_payCaller`, `buyback`, the sale pushes, the book push, `migrate`, `rescueCoin` (coin), `rescueNft` (no credit, no held statement), `swapCredit` (credit for an equal or higher score credit), `sellCreditTo` (gross at least cost). each is bounded as SPEC 11 lists; the balance token bound is par (I1).

## unconfirmed

- U1, the profit size of M2: needs a fork test that seeds a native eth / balance token v4 pool with realistic liquidity, sets the pool key, turns the bid on, warps the balance rate to par, then in one transaction swaps the pool down, buys past window pile credits, sells them into `sellForBalance` and swaps back. profit is the tokens at the restored price less the eth paid and the two swaps.
- U2, compose liveness: a credit that `Statements.compose` refuses, placed in the oldest page through `sellForEth` or `swapCredit`, would stall `compose()` on that page while `composeChosen` is off. needs a read of the mainnet Statements compose requirements. the `sellForEth` path predates this diff.

verdict: not fit to freeze. M1 breaks a stated floor guarantee in immutable Core library code with a fix of a few lines, and M2 needs a Core level guard that cannot be added after deployment; both sit in the balance bid, which ships switched off, so the fixes cost a short packet and a rerun of the touched suites.
