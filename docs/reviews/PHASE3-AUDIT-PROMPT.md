## What is being audited

Five new contracts to be deployed beside an already live, unchanged Core on Ethereum mainnet, the scripts that deploy and wire them, and the keeper door that feeds one of them.

- `src/adapters/BalanceTreasuryV2.sol`: the Core's next balance module and the custodian of the BALANCE token (ERC20, 18 decimals, `0x3620540c994433D7B4a628C46d2ED5F92572eA07`). It faces the Core with the v1 surface (`unitPerPoint` 1e14, `available`, `pay`, `redeem`). Custody is split into earmarks (recorded debt `totalOwed`, spender buckets `totalBuckets`, committed payouts `totalCommitted`) and working funds; `cushion` is the part of working funds that `sweep`, `allocate` and `withdrawWorking` may not take. Every rule that spends the backing is a registered spender (`SpenderState` in `src/interfaces/Interfaces.sol`): a redeemer, quoted through `IRedeemer` in `redeemCC` against a CC burn, or a module that pays through `draw(to, amount, accrue)`. The treasury holds no pricing rule. `migrateTo(successor)` is a last resort that repeats the v1 handshake. The live v1 treasury it replaces is `src/adapters/Phase2BalanceTreasury.sol` at `0xB85CF439Ca57b4bfC28fA1Cb8a97Ee85555B0496`.
- `src/adapters/CreditBid.sol`: the BALANCE bid, a spender module. Buys the caller's Credits at `bidBps` of par (80 to 100 percent, climbing while idle, dropping per 100 points filled), capped so the payout's eth value at the controller's protected sale price is at most the Core's eth price for the credit plus `overEthBps`, refuses credits the controller sold inside the Core's return lock, transfers the credits to the Core, `adopt`s them into the pile, then draws the seller's payout exactly and the artist royalty with accrual from its own bucket.
- `src/adapters/ProRataRedeemer.sol` and `src/adapters/MarketRedeemer.sol`: redeemers, reviewed before; in scope for how they interact with the renamed spender surface.
- `src/ControllerV3.sol`: the next controller (compare with the live `src/ControllerV2.sol`). New in these commits: the market floor reference (`MarketFloor` struct, `setMarketFloor`, `postMarketFloor`, `marketFloorAsk`, `StaleFloor`) and its term in `_creditPrice`: the credit sale floor is `max(points floor, core cost floor, posted floor x (10_000 + spreadBps) / 10_000)`, and a credit the term lifts is not for sale while the post is older than `maxStale`.
- `src/interfaces/Interfaces.sol` (`BalanceBid` without the per call and per period caps, `SpenderState`, `MarketFloor`) and the generated interfaces `IBalanceTreasuryV2.sol`, `ICreditBid.sol`, `IControllerV3.sol` (`script/tools/gen-interfaces.sh --check` must be clean).
- Scripts: `script/Phase3.s.sol` (`deploy` 5 creations, `migrate`, `bid`, `ens`, `readback`), the pins in `script/Checks.sol` (`BUILD_HASH_CONTROLLER_V3`, `BUILD_HASH_TREASURY_V2`, `BUILD_HASH_CREDIT_BID`) and `test/BuildIdentity.t.sol`.
- Off chain: the keeper's `postMarketFloor` door (`keeper/src/decide.ts` `postMarketFloorNeeded`, `keeper/src/chain.ts` `readControllerV3`, `keeper/src/main.ts` `collectionBid`, `keeper/src/exec.ts`, `keeper/src/config.ts`), the OpenSea collection offer read `fetchCollectionBid` in `packages/engine/src/opensea.mjs`, and the ABIs in `packages/engine/src/abis.ts` (`controllerV3Abi`, `treasuryV2Abi`, `creditBidAbi`) against the artifacts.

Read for integration, do not audit for changes (they are unchanged and deployed): `src/Core.sol` (`0xcdb1889ae12074d750CD9B8531B692015465e0dD`), `src/lib/CoreLib.sol`, `src/lib/CoreOpsLib.sol`, `src/ControllerV2.sol` (`0xCD64e368b3d7033B2E2B22Fa5F67f554aB807d36`), `src/adapters/Phase2BalanceTreasury.sol`, the BALANCE token, Credits (`0x97630aa70ab14ed9883b41dafccbc11349723043`), Statements and the CreditScore contract (`0x817A9cFfb4d6E7c206e745A4229001A472C1b7B7`), the coin, the push split at `0x0493299d3384518bFAb5b061384271ed771EDDE2` (the artist recipient). ## Properties to break

Attack each one. For every property either show a concrete path (inputs, state, sequence, the exact line) or state precisely why none exists.

1. Custody. BALANCE leaves treasury v2 only through: `pay` to the Core's recipient bounded by `available()` (working funds, only while working payments are on and v2 is the selected module of a live Core); `claim` of recorded debt; `draw` by a registered, on, active spender from its own bucket inside its per call, per period and lifetime caps while v2 is the module and not paused; `redeemCC` against an actual CC burn from the quoted redeemer's own bucket inside the same caps; `release` of a committed payout at its vesting; `withdrawWorking` of working funds above the cushion; `migrateTo` to a verified successor. The cushion is never taken by `sweep`, `allocate` or `withdrawWorking`. No bucket pays from another, from the debt, from the committed payouts or from the cushion. `solvent()` (custody at or above every earmark) holds after every reachable sequence, including a transfer that delivers less than `amount`, a recipient that reverts or burns gas, and reentry through the token, the Statements artwork callback, a redeemer's `quote`, a spender's `draw` and the Core's `adopt`.
2. The spender door. A malicious or buggy registered spender can take at most its bucket, at most `perCall` per draw, `perPeriod` per 24 hours from the period start and `lifetime` in all, to a recipient of its choice, only after its one day activation and only while on. State the exact blast radius of a compromised `CreditBid` and of a compromised keeper (which is not a spender). `drawable()` equals what `draw` would allow. The period reset, the uint128 casts and the epoch bump on `unallocate` cannot be used to exceed a cap. `accrue` true records exactly the undelivered part as `owed` and the `ACCRUE_RESERVE` gas keeps the record reachable under the Core's and the module's gas bounds; `accrue` false reverts on any short delivery. The treasury's transient lock and the module's own lock do not deadlock the legitimate path (module `sellCredit` → Core `adopt` → treasury `draw` twice) and do not leave a reentry window between the two draws.
3. The credit bid. The payout is never above par (`score x unitPerPoint`), never above the eth cap at the controller's protected sale value, and `bidBpsNow` stays strictly under `floorMultBps()` at each fill so a credit the engine resells after its return lock is worth more at the engine than at the bid. The return lock refusal reads `creditSoldAt` from the live controller: state what a seller can do with credits ControllerV2 sold before the controller switch (no V3 record) and whether the one day spender activation after `migrate` covers it. Credits transferred to the Core before `adopt` cannot be stranded without payment, and a seller cannot be paid for a credit that did not land in the pile. The two draws (seller exact, artist with accrual) cannot be split so that the seller is paid and the artist royalty silently fails a cap. `setBid` while the bid is on, a cap raised by the owner above the Core's `balanceBidCapBps`, and `floorMultBps` falling after `setBid` are all safe. The module owns no BALANCE and no Credits at rest. Anyone can call `sweep`: state what repeated sweeps do to `available()` and whether the cushion is the right bound.
4. Migration in both directions. v1 → v2 is the deployed v1 `migrateTo` handshake: v2 must already be the Core's module, bind the same coin and token, name v1 as its immutable `predecessor`, have every switch off and `ccRedemptionEnabled()` false (every spender off), `migrated()` false; `receiveMigration` fills the pro rata bucket only when custody covers every earmark plus the import. v2 → successor: `migrateTo` requires every switch and spender off, every bucket but the pro rata one returned (`totalBuckets == pro rata bucket`), moves custody less debt and committed payouts, exports the pro rata bucket, sets `migrated` before transferring, and v2 then refuses `setSwitches` and `setSpenderOn(true)` forever while `claim` and `release` keep paying. Attack the successor checks with a hostile successor contract. State what happens to the Core's exchange between `setBalanceModule(v2)` and `v2.setSwitches(true, true)` and whether anything can be lost in that window.
5. The market floor reference. `setMarketFloor` bounds (`spreadBps` at most 10,000, `stepBps` in 1 to 10,000, `minSeconds` nonzero); `postMarketFloor` by the poster is clamped to one `stepBps` step from the last post and paced to one post per `minSeconds`, the owner is neither; a non poster reverts `OnlyPoster`; `maxStale` 0 switches the term off; `marketFloorAsk` is `(0, true)` when off or never posted. Quantify a compromised keeper key: how far can it move the floor per hour given step 1,000 bps and cadence 600 seconds, what does a floor of 1 wei do (the term drops out), what does a very high floor do (lifted credits unsellable, no loss), and what does a keeper going dark do (`StaleFloor` only on the credits the term lifts; the points floor still sells the rest). Confirm the term cannot lower any ask below what it is today, cannot affect the Core's eth bid, the BALANCE exchange, composition or Statement pricing, and that `buyCredit` and `buyCredits` revert cleanly on a stale lifted credit with no state change. The uint128 and uint40 casts.
6. ControllerV3 integration. The Core's probes (`nextPage`, `atFloor`, `pastWindow`, `priceOf`, `valuePerPoint`, `balanceClampFor`, `markupBps`) still behave and fail closed with the new storage; `floorMultBps` reads the live `balanceBidCapBps` and `creditSpreadBps` (the live ControllerV2 `spreadBps` is being raised to 2,500 before the switch, and `deploy` copies the live value: confirm every bound and test that assumed 1.1 still holds at 1.25, and name the ones that do not). Runtime size under 24,576 bytes with headroom.
7. Deployment and wiring. `Phase3.deploy` creates with plain CREATE and `CreditBid` is constructed on the treasury it was deployed with; `migrate` is in the right order (v1 off, `setBalanceModule(v2)`, v1 `migrateTo`, cushion, the bid and redeemer 2 registered as spenders with caps and shares, the band on the module, switches on, `sweep`, V3 pool keys, CC quote, Statement sales, market floor set and posted, `setController(V3)`), every send is inside `vm.startBroadcast` and refused when the signer is not the owner, the preview refuses on a wrong binding before any send, and `readback` checks the pinned masked runtime hashes against the live code. `bid` is `setSpenderOn(bid, true)`; confirm it is refused before the spender's activation. `ens` sends `setAddr` of `vault.creditcycle.eth` (registry owner the Core owner, unwrapped, resolver `0xF29100983E058B709F3D539b0c765937B804AC15`) only once v2 is the module; state what a resolver swap or a wrapped name would do. No step can send with a decoded preview missing, and no step prints a secret.
8. The keeper door. `fetchCollectionBid` takes OpenSea's best collection offer: trait offers excluded, ETH or WETH only, 18 decimals, per item when the offer's consideration quantity is above one; state what a crafted or malformed API response can make it return and whether a wrong bid can reach the controller unclamped. `postMarketFloorNeeded` mirrors the contract's clamp, honors `minSeconds`, posts on a move above `FLOOR_TOL_BPS` or past half of `maxStale`, never posts without an observation, and drops an observation older than three refresh periods. The door is unpaid and under the refresh door's gas cap; the `postMarketFloor` call goes to the controller with the V3 abi; the key never leaves the keeper. State the failure modes of a dead OpenSea API, a rate limited one and a key revoked mid run.
9. Build identity and ABIs. The pinned hashes in `script/Checks.sol` equal the masked runtime hashes of the artifacts (`test/BuildIdentity.t.sol`, 15 artifacts); the generated interfaces equal the artifacts; `packages/engine/src/abis.ts` entries for ControllerV3, treasury v2 and CreditBid match the artifacts' selectors and event signatures (a wrong tuple layout in `spenders` or a wrong event would mis-decode silently).
10. Economics as implemented. The bid never pays above par (decision 285) and the floor term never asks under the collection bid plus spread (decision 291); the sweep shares (25 percent bid, 75 percent market redeemer, 0 pro rata) and the 150,000 cushion decide how much of the ~570,000 BALANCE can ever become bid or redemption budget and how fast; the 10 percent conversion contribution keeps funding the pro rata bucket that is switched off. State any way a party extracts value from a bucket beyond its rule, any way the owner's listed powers do something the docs do not disclose, and whether the loop (bid buys, pile composes, Statement converts into working funds, surplus swept back into buckets) can run at a loss the cushion does not stop.
