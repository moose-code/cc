### Setter bounds, ordering and locks checked

- `Core.setSettings` (`SettingsBounds.sol:11-42`) checks drop **1–1,000 bps**, minute floor **5,000–10,000**, climb **1–1,000**, ceiling **10,000–30,000**, idle loosen **1–2,000**, sale floor **1,000–40,000**, sale-to-buyback **0–10,000**, artist and operator cuts **0–1,000 each**, peak half life **600–604,800 s**, recovery climb **climb–1,000**, recovery gap **0–5,000**, buyback slice **0.01–1 ETH** and at least twice maximum keeper payment, delay **1–7,200 blocks**, keeper fee **0–0.005 ETH**, keeper max base fee **1–100 gwei**, rate cap **10¹³–10¹⁸ wei**, redeem-burn fee share **0–10,000**, caller fee **0–0.005 ETH**, caller max base fee **0–100 gwei**, redemption slippage **0–1,000**, balance margin **0–9,000**, reserve release share **0–10,000**, payee share **0–200,000 ppm**, `refScore` **800,000–8,000,000**, and swap fee **0–10²⁴ coin wei**. It also refuses a nonzero artist cut with no artist and a sale floor above the current controller markup. Before a controller exists, that cross-field check is skipped; the controller's own setter checks the other direction once installed. `setRate` requires **10¹³–10¹⁸ wei** and no more than `rateCap`; `setCreditCostToPotBps` allows **0–10,000**; `setMinCcToChoose` accepts any `uint96`; the chosen-compose and pause setters are booleans.
- `ControllerV1.setSale` (`ControllerV1.sol:129-150`) bounds markup **10,000–40,000 bps** and at least the current Core sale floor, start cap **markup–200,000**, decay **1–720 h**, anchor half life **1–90 days**, and minimum anchor cost **0–10 ETH**. `setAnchorBps` takes **10,000–startCapBps**. `setCredit` bounds its window **1 minute–30 days**, floor **10,000–40,000 bps**, and start multiplier **10,000–200,000 bps**; its sale switch is boolean. `setBalancePoolKey` needs an initialized native-ETH/balance-token pool and an already set balance token.
- `Core.setController` requires deployed code and a bounded `nextPage` probe, and `lockController` requires a nonzero controller. `setBalanceModule` requires code, a valid unit and `available` response, a contract token, and the same token as any prior module; installing it while the bid is on resets the bid to its floor. `lockBalanceModule` requires a module. `addAdapter` and `setSuccessor` reject protected targets; `removeAdapter` remains open after `lockAdapters`, while `lockSuccessor` can seal a zero successor. `setPayee` and `setArtist` reject forbidden recipients, and a nonzero artist cut prevents a zero artist. `transferOwnership` is a two-step handover. `releaseRedeemBurn` is capped by the reserve; `rescueCoin` moves only coin, `rescueNft` never moves Credits or a held statement, and `migrate` moves only the ETH pot and pile credits.
- `setPaused` stops statement/credit sales and `swapCredit`; it does not stop bids, compose, redemption, fee booking, or buyback. `pausedTotal` excludes paused seconds from the controller's sale clocks. Launcher factory `setMinProtocolSkimShareBps` is owner-only and bounded **0–10,000**; the required **1,000** must precede coin launch. After launch, coin admin can change allowed transfer addresses or unrestrict until `lockAllowlist`, and repoint hook bounty until `lockRecipients` or admin renounce. These two coin locks are separate from Core's four one-way locks.
