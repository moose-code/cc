## Decision and limits

The proposed **0.00465 ETH** follows the recent completed-sale median. Decision 60 calls for an opening bid at the market price, and decision 145 made the unit ETH per 100 points with a deployment-day market refresh. The cheapest standing ask is higher. At 440 points, the proposed bid quotes 0.02046 ETH to a seller; the 2.5% on-top artist royalty makes the pot cost 0.0209715 ETH before any keeper payment. The candidate's 0.0049 ETH would quote 0.02156 ETH to that seller, 5.10% more. No sampled strategy ask clears at either opening rate; direct willing sellers and later bid climb remain necessary for initial acquisitions. An advertised OpenSea ask may clear later but its fulfillment is unverified here.

This check uses a 2.56-hour sale window, not the intended trailing 24 hours, and a listing snapshot that expires quickly. It does not establish wash-trade resistance, sale provenance for every item, net transaction cost, production keeper inclusion or order fulfillment. Before mainnet broadcast, pull the full trailing-24-hour fulfilled-sale set from an authenticated marketplace or independently decoded sale index, recompute the score-normalized median, inspect executable listings again, then sign the exact rate, 3x cap and resulting config hash. The provisional pair remains subject to that review; this report authorizes no transaction.
