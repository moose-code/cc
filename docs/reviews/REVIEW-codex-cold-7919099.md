# Cold second audit of `7919099`

Target: `7919099f912ae94b85e20285edb0ddd02be7da7c` on main. Review branch `codex/cold-audit-7919099` changes tests and documentation only. No contract behavior, mainnet transaction, deployment or broadcast was changed or sent.

## Verdict

**The shipped-off launch path starts and keeps acquiring when market trades fund it and the keeper sees profitable listings.** With the actual launch rate `0.0049 ETH per 100 points`, `0.1 ETH` reserve, credit sale on and the deployed Artcoins v2 hook, a fresh Core had `ethPot = 0` and received unbooked fees from real swaps. The bid rose without a prior engine call, the first keeper purchase booked the income, the payee latched after 1,800 seconds, and 93 later keeper books kept the pot current. The 24-hour test ended with six acquired Credits and a `5.139066157599939746 ETH` booked pot. A chain with no trade or other income correctly has a zero payable bid at 0, 10 minutes, 2 hours and 24 hours. A listing exactly at the capped bid has no gas-paying gap and the keeper leaves it idle; a market seller can still use the bid door.

**Value mode is unsafe to turn on with the stated reference values.** A real v4 pool swap and reverse swap in one block made an ETH Credit sell directly profitable after pool fees and fork base-fee gas. A second real-pool test lowered the price of an engine Credit purchase by more than those costs. The instantaneous liquidity gate can be satisfied temporarily in the same block. Separately, a due `refreshReference` can update the reference while paying the keeper zero from an empty booked pot, even though the keeper projects payment from real unbooked hook fees. Once the reference is stale, `ethRate()` reverts `NoQuote` until a refresh is funded or a non-profit caller intervenes. The exact profits below depend on the test pool's `1e18` active liquidity, 3,000 fee tier and synthetic BALANCE token; they are a reproducible attack path, not an estimate of the undeployed production BALANCE market.

The first audit is available verbatim at `docs/reviews/REVIEW-codex-496f2f9.md` in later docs-only commit `5494cce`; `git diff --stat 7919099 5494cce` shows that file as the sole change, so contract target `7919099` remains exact. I compared its S1, S2, S4, S5, I1 to I4, L1 to L4, and B1 to B6 with the findings below. S1 and the launch stack/fixture/integration repairs were addressed by the PRs named in its header. Its S5 is still present and appears as C05 with a new reproducer, explicitly **not** a newly discovered issue. Its S2/L2 balance depeg concern remains an activation consideration; the later value-reference packet aims to price against BALANCE but introduces C01 to C04. Its S4 owner/controller trust limit and B2, B5 and B6 remain documented limitations rather than new cold-state findings. `docs/reviews/FIXTURE-AUDIT-f7e55fa.md` was also read and its cold-start gap list checked against the present harnesses.

## Cold state and external inflows

The new `test_launchValuesColdViewsEqualFirstBook` starts from deploy, generates fee income with a real Artcoins v2 pool buy and leaves it unbooked. At ages `0`, `600`, `7,200` and `86,400` seconds, its ETH rates are respectively `0.0049`, `0.0057134`, `0.0092708` and `0.0147 ETH per 100 points`. At each age, `ethRate`, `bidOf`, `bidForScore`, `floorRate`, `climbMultiplier`, `spendState` and `valueOf` exactly match the same-block result after first `book`. `balanceRate = 9,000 bps`, `balanceBidOf = 0`, `ethToBuyback = 0`, `referencePrice = 0`, and `nextPage.ready = false` stay the same because no module, sale or reference exists. `creditPrice`, `priceOf` and `atFloor` revert `NotForSale` before and after the book when there is no inventory. The separate zero-trade test confirms `ethRate`, both bids and booked pot stay zero through the same ages. The general fixture's `0.001732 ETH` matrix also passes with `1.602 ETH` projected first-bucket budget, but it is not used to claim launch numbers.

Real hook trades in these tests include a `0.005 ETH` small push, `2 ETH` cold push, the launch day's `3 + 2 ETH` burst with a coin sell, and `0.05 ETH` buys every five minutes. The Core's `receive` accepts the deployed hook and escrow; `_book` credits untracked ETH at its next pot-reading door. A stranger's `0.3 ETH` escrow claim was verified by `test_strangerEscrowClaimLandsAsIncomeForTheNextBook`: it lands unbooked, then a book with the test's 50% redeem-burn share sends `0.15 ETH` to that pot and `0.15 ETH` to `ethPot`. The forced-ETH-only focused test passes: forced ETH above the tracked pots is treated as income and its first sell pays the quote. A balance token donated to the module changes live `available()` and the next balance bid, as the focused `BalanceBid` tests verify; a donation to Core is C06. A Credit transferred directly to Core is recovered by the permissionless `adopt` path and does not need `rescueNft`; an untracked Statement can be rescued, while a held one cannot. Those focused `Adopt` and `Rescue` tests pass.

Time-only checks include the corrected 24-hour launch with fees dripped while the keeper idles between gates and new `test_midIdleRealTradesGrowMultiplierBeforeAnyFill`: two mid-idle real hook pushes raised `climbMultiplier` from 10,000 to 15,991 bps without a fill or engine call, and the first book preserved the displayed multiplier and rate. The focused launch-drop peak-halving test, the eight-day statement anchor decay without a buyer, and the balance `available()` rise before any balance fill. `reserve()` is `max(0.1 ETH, 1.5 times queued caller payments)` at launch; the focused Reserve tests pass with a shrinking pot, and the cold matrix keeps the bid below pages pot less reserve. The sniper boundary latches on the first income-bearing book at or after `1,800` seconds, paying the new payee share only on later books. Its default `payeePpm` is zero; C02 still matters if raised or if refresh is economically required in value mode.

## Keeper launch day, hour by hour

`LaunchDay` calls `_keeperTick` each 12-second block through 30 minutes, each 60-second block through 6 hours and each 300-second block through 24 hours. Only the keeper calls automation doors. Real market actors make swaps, genuine Seaport and CreditStrategy listings, and two organic Credit sells. The test applies the keeper's 120-second continuous listing delay, gas and gap gate, payee-latch book rule and after-latch rule of pending income **above** `0.01 ETH` and more than 600 seconds since its last book. It asserts a sample at each of the 24 hour boundaries and emits every exact bid, booked pot, pending amount and pile count.

At hour 1: bid `0.009392784596263900 ETH/100 points`, booked pot `4.098473547843328689 ETH`, pile 3. At hour 2: bid reaches the `0.0147 ETH` cap, pot `4.128940691799939746`, pending `0.0045`, pile 4. At hour 3: pot `4.150542968799939746`, pile 5. Hours 4 through 6 have pot `4.199142968799939746 + 0.0486 * (hour - 4) ETH` and pile 5. Hour 7 has pot `4.312866157599939746`, pile 6. Hours 8 through 24 have pot `4.361466157599939746 + 0.0486 * (hour - 8) ETH` and pile 6. The bid stays `0.0147` from hour 2 onward; sampled pending income is zero except at hour 2. The keeper booked post-latch income 93 times. `nextPage` remained unready because six Credits are below the required 80. A real Seaport ask exactly equal to the capped `0.0147 ETH` bid stayed unswept for ten minutes: its gap is zero, so keeper gas and margin cannot clear. That is expected from `keeper/src/decide.ts`, not a contract failure.

The keeper's `refreshReference` path is absent from this shipped-off launch because no balance module or reference is configured. C02 is the value-mode first-hour counterexample: its separate TypeScript oracle test predicts paid refresh from pending income, while the real Core pays zero or one booked wei. A keeper that enforces profit by measured receipt will stop; one that only trusts the projection will lose gas. `NoQuote` then closes ETH bids after `referenceMaxStale`.

## Value mode bounds, seams and test realism

The real-v4 `test_realPoolSpotSpikeAndEveryBlockReferenceWalk` starts with a `0.49 ETH` per BALANCE token reference, swaps `3 ETH` to move spot above `13.623480999999999981 ETH` per token, and calls refresh every 12-second block for an hour. Exactly six calls move the reference: each at the 600-second cadence, each at most 200 bps, ending at `0.551819585439360000 ETH` per token, a compounded `12.6162419264%` rise. With launch `tolBps = 450` and hard bid cap `11,000 bps`, the maximum quoted price for **100 points** is approximately `0.005745201 ETH` after one due step and `0.00634316613462543 ETH` after six; `rateCap = 0.0147 ETH` remains an independent ceiling. Before any due step the band ceiling is `0.00563255 ETH/100 points`. Per credit, multiply by its actual `score / 1,000,000`; a 100-point Credit has score `1,000,000`. The per-block aggregate payout is bounded by booked plus pending pot less reserve and the first-fill/active-bucket spend rules; without a bound on new market fee inflow, there is no finite ETH total per hour independent of pot growth. In the test's fixed `10 ETH` pot with `0.1 ETH` reserve and no new income, at most `9.9 ETH` can leave through acquisitions over any hour, while each Credit's price remains subject to the per-score cap and compounding drop. These are damage bounds, not protection from C01.

The new mode-off test uses a real v4 pool, sets `referenceMaxStale = 0`, waits one day, moves spot with a swap, and verifies `refreshReference()` is inert and a Credit sale still executes. The existing `ModeOffDifferential` and `ValueMode` focused suites pass; `ValueMode` mostly writes the pool's price and liquidity slots with `vm.store`, so its pass proves formulas and revert gates, not resistance to a traded market. The new real-pool tests cover the seed guard, instantaneous liquidity, 10x spike, cadence, same-block buy and sell, and mode-off zero-stale path. The new real-pool low-unit test verifies `valueOf(1) = 1 wei`, `valueOf(2) = 1 wei` and `valueOf(3) = 2 wei` at a `0.49 ETH` spot and module unit 1. The new spend-cap test rejects 10,000 bps while value mode is on and retains 9,999. Existing tests cover switch-off reset and the loop-closure bound: with `tolBps = 450`, the minimum spread is 943 bps, so the launch `1,000` passes; the proposed value-mode 200 does not. The new real-pool quote-outage test advances 3,601 seconds past a one-hour reference expiry: `ethRate()` reverts `NoQuote`, but a balance fill remains live and records `0.0093143169 ETH` cost basis rather than one wei. Door-by-door gas and every new revert path against a compiled pre-packet binary were not measured; exact pre-packet equivalence cannot be claimed from the differential suite alone. An `off` controller buy still enters `refreshReference()` and returns early when max stale is zero, which adds a call path even when pricing is unchanged.

`forge inspect` on this commit reports Core `24,273` bytes, `303` under EIP-170, and CoreLib `24,525`, only `51` under. Core and its linked library are not proxies; their code and library link cannot be replaced after deployment. The owner can change settings and rate, replace ControllerV1 until `lockController`, replace the balance module until `lockBalanceModule`, and move tracked ETH and pile Credits to a successor after setting it, but that is a migration, not an in-place CoreLib patch. A replacement controller can change price policy; it cannot make CoreLib's C02 refresh book income or pay from the projected pot. The shipped-off seam is therefore a poor permanent freeze boundary for value-mode activation. Resolve C01, C02 and C04 and rerun the configured frozen-candidate gates before deploying or switching mode on.
