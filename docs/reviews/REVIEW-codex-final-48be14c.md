**Correction audit, C01-C08.** The original `ColdAudit` 23 tests passed, including real-pool same-block sell and buy manipulation, one-block JIT, pending and post-latch refresh payments, view/door equality at due refresh, thin-module zero quote, low-rate rounding, seed guard, value-mode spend-cap rejection and outage cost basis. `ViewEqualsStored` passed 6/6, `ModeOffDifferential` 5/5, `ValueMode` 23/23, `ReturnLock` 9/9, `V2Port` 8/8 and `GasCap` 13/13. Thus C01's *same-block* fix works but S01/S02 cross the refresh boundary; C04's one-block fix works but S03 uses two transient samples. C02's Core refresh now books pending income before payment, including after the payee latch, and its ETH bid fallback stays callable; C03 views project a due refresh; C05 quotes zero for an unpayable thin module; C07's phase-one launch fixture pins the launch values; C08's 86-Credit mode-off batch fits at `16,220,812` estimated gas. C06's broad stray-ERC20 rescue works, but does not solve a direct balance-token donation (S05).

**Outflow and control-path review.** `sellForEth` both overloads, `buyListing` through the Strategy and Seaport adapters, `buyback`, `redeemStatement`, `migrate`, artist/operator/payee transfers, caller payments, `rescueToken` and `rescueNft` were checked against `Adapters` 14/14, `ReviewDeploy` 10/10, `Rescue` 8/8, `ReturnLock` 9/9, `Migrate` 18/18, `ColdAudit`, the two launch suites and the 28-check rehearsal. The buyer return lock is per buyer and timestamp packed in one word; a transfer to a different owner is intentionally unlocked. The reserve is withheld room, not a separate ETH account. An ETH acquisition's first fill in a bucket can exceed the nominal percentage budget; the launch cap is off at 10,000 bps, and the 0.1 ETH reserve is the immediate pot floor. The `buyback` slice must cover twice the maximum keeper payment; listing payment is bounded by the bid gap and room. The delegatecalled CoreLib shares State slot zero and uses the same reentrancy-guard storage; Core doors that call untrusted tokens, modules, adapters or ETH recipients are guarded, while library-forwarded rescue and migration enter the guard themselves. I found no additional reproducible reentrancy or overpayment path in those checks. Owner can change all economic settings at once, replace the controller or module until their one-way locks, and migrate to a successor; a malicious owner remains the trust assumption.

**Value-mode bound and limitation.** At `1,200` bps/hour, step `200` bps, minimum `600` seconds, the reference can rise at most 2% per due refresh, about `12.6162419264%` compounded over six due refreshes in one hour. The older real-pool 10x spike test measured the reference from `0.49` to `0.55181958543936` ETH per token in an hour; the corresponding 100-point bid ceiling was about `0.005745201` ETH after one due step and `0.00634316613462543` after six, independently limited by `rateCap = 0.0147` ETH per 100 points. A fixed 10 ETH pot with a 0.1 ETH reserve exposes at most 9.9 ETH to acquisitions over that hour; new fee income removes any finite pot-independent hourly ETH bound. That bound does not make the per-trade S01/S02 discounts safe. Mode off uses the previous pricing rules and `referenceMaxStale = 0` skips observation; the switch-off reset and loop-closure setting check passed. No mainnet value-mode activation is warranted by the candidate.

**Launch values and exposure.** The candidate and config match the chosen mechanisms: opening rate is to be pulled for 100 points on deploy morning; cap is 3x; drop `115` bps per 100 points; climb and recovery `200` bps/minute; no meaningful drop floor (`dropFloorBps = 1`); spend cap off (`10,000`); reserve `0.1` ETH; return lock `21,600` seconds; credit spread `1,000` bps; value mode off. These are scenario outcomes, not a bound on adversarial flow: `EXPOSURE-148` refills the ask-book pot every block and assumes a seller distribution and market ask. The absence of a spend cap allows a first-block wave, with the 0.1 ETH reserve as the immediate pot floor. An owner must explicitly accept that exposure and refresh the market opening rate before launch.

**Combined fixes by severity.**

1. **High, activation blockers:** replace the sampled spot with a sustained, manipulation-resistant price and depth measure for both ETH bids and Credit/Statement asks (S01-S03). Keep value mode off until real production-pool economics and the loop bound are retested.
2. **Medium, release evidence and operations:** index a paid reference refresh (S04); make phase-two fees stay pending until an actual actor books (B02); keep all five corrected stale candidate assertions (B03); either use the pinned production balance token in the full rehearsal or state the split gate plainly (R01); correct the Core/SPEC/RUNBOOK/INTEGRATION false instructions (L01-L04); record the deploy-morning market price and 3x cap (R02).
3. **Low, custody and cost:** resolve or explicitly accept unrecoverable direct BALANCE donations before Core bytecode is frozen (S05); improve one-credit gas while retaining the 86-credit cap (B01); update STATUS after the release decision (L05).

**What changes when.** Before deploying Core/CoreLib, fix or explicitly accept S05's direct-balance-token custody gap; before launching the indexer, fix S04's refresh snapshot trigger. Correct the operational false statements L01-L04 before using those documents for deployment.
