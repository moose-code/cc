# Third audit: launch candidate 1db4a9f

Target: main commit 1db4a9fc3267ae246a4317d56a881818554e16ba, in a fresh clone on audit branch codex/final-audit-1db4a9f. No production contract behavior was changed. Fork tests used Ethereum chain ID 1 at mainnet block 26,208,025 and the deployed Artcoins v2 factory 0xC27e182ef5D8A1A7C9dd0a27414fEaBA47DF8e3c and hook 0x866f31562dDE70d7b989C2529A9DEfFfFd3bE8cc, whose record identifies source d27c932f. The pinned Jack token was fetched and built with script/tools/upstream-balance.sh at 78f3a3132725af5c1fbfb1c1d5cf09b7ccb12e16; its creation and runtime hashes matched the script pins. All broadcasts in this audit targeted local Anvil forks. No mainnet transaction was sent.

**Verdict:** The phase-one engine starts and moves under real trading fees and keeper-only automation. The candidate is not ready for the configured release gate: the pinned real-token royalty transfer fails, the upstream integration suite fails, and the freeze table still names an older candidate. Before the balance route or value mode is enabled, the royalty liability, quote-outage payout, and ETH-pot coupling need structural treatment.

**Launch values.** script/config/mainnet.json still specifies a deploy-morning rateStart of 0.0049 ETH per 100 points and a 3x cap at 0.0147, drop 115 bps per 100 points, climb and recovery climb 200 bps/min, drop floor 1 bps (effectively no floor), spend cap 10,000 (off), reserve 0.1 ETH, return lock 21,600 seconds, credit spread 1,000 bps, purchase royalty 250 bps, balance discount 1,000 bps, quote tolerance 100 bps, value mode off and fee-to-redeem-burn 0. The deployed-morning market pull has not happened in this audit; 0.0049 and its 3x cap are placeholders until that pull. For a 433-point Credit, the opening seller bid is 0.021217 ETH and the 2.5 percent on-top royalty makes pot cost 0.021747425 ETH. At the same rate, one ETH buys about 19,910 points including the royalty, versus about 20,408 without it, 2.44 percent fewer. The ETH purchase cost basis includes the royalty, so a cost-based credit sale floor rises with that extra 0.000530425 ETH on the example Credit. The updated simulator's 0.809146 ETH all-in dump at a 2 ETH pot is directionally consistent with the prior exposure trade-off, but the historical grid cannot be used as the exact candidate bound until regenerated.

**Before any mainnet deploy:** resolve S01 in the immutable path or in a module with enforceable royalty liabilities, make the real-token suite green, decide and encode safe quote-failure behavior for future value mode, regenerate the candidate exposure figures, complete the exact-SHA freeze gates and the deploy-morning rate pull. The current check-freeze.sh failure and required upstream test failure are release blockers. The REST endpoint and vulnerable web dependencies should be repaired before a public web launch. The other ETH purchase and keeper settings remain owner-adjustable; rateStart and constructor/code seams do not.

**Before balance-route activation:** deploy and bind Jack's exact pinned token/module, register its renderer, finalize retirement, wait for transfersOpenBlock, and verify artist royalty receipt or reserved claim with the real token. Keep module available() honest about unpaid liabilities. Before value-mode activation, also correct S02 and S03, choose minLiquidity from live sustainable depth, set all four pending reference parameters, confirm two healthy samples and the 600-second cadence, keep spendCapBps below 10,000, satisfy the 203-bps minimum spread at 100-bps tolerance, and test cross-wallet round trips and quote outages at the actual launch pool. Do not infer net attack profitability from the synthetic 0.49 ETH/token pool.

**Cold-start answer:** Yes for the ETH engine with only market trades and the keeper: fee pushes are unbooked, the views rise, the keeper books and buys, and the later page and buyback doors fire. No for switching on the shipped balance/value-mode pair as-is: the real BALANCE royalty is not paid, an unusable quote opens the posted balance payout, and zero ETH room can silence a funded balance module.
