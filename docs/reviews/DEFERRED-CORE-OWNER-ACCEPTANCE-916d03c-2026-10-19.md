## Explicitly accepted limits for this Core-only step

The complete same-record end-to-end lifecycle remains unproven. The signed local run used an impersonated, pre-funded factory owner and test signer, and its later swaps, fee booking and one strategy acquisition were an in-process follow-up. `ripe0x` signer identity is checked when the keystore is unlocked; it has not been proven by the encrypted keystore metadata. The public Tenderly RPC parsed an invalid raw-send probe and served the wrapper's required reads, but a valid signed mainnet send has not been tested. The Etherscan key supplied in chat is stored in macOS Keychain for verification and should be rotated because it was exposed. Hosted keeper, indexer, website binding and alerts cannot be accepted until there is a real deployment record and remain gates for coin launch and production operation.

The owner chose to proceed with the **deferred Core** despite these limits. The `e2e` and final review rows in the freeze table are recorded as **owner waivers for this stage**, not passed tests or independent acceptance. The later coin and pool launch requires its own review, service checks and transaction confirmation. The deployment script must still show the full config, current gas ceiling and each decoded transaction and stop on a mismatch or failed receipt.
