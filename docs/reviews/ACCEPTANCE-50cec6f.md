## part 3, owner goals at launch settings

Numbers use P0 = 0.0089 eth per credit (rateStart), a page of 80 = 0.712 eth, sale split 50 percent to the coin buyback.

- G1 start 1.5x, floor 75 percent: holds at anchor 1 (hour 50 at 100 percent, hour 150 at 75 percent). `exitAfter` is 378000 s (105 h): once an exit module is set, anyone can pull a held eth statement at hour 105 when its ask is 86.25 percent, so the 75 percent floor is unreachable for statements the exit takes first (docs/RUNBOOK.md:491 says decide it again at `setExitModule`). With anchor above 1 the ask at hour 105 is still above 100 percent: anchor 2 starts at 300 percent, 195 percent at hour 105.
- G2 burns from sales and credits flowing: `saleToBuybackBps` 5000 sends half of every sale to the coin burn. Return to the pot per unit of cost: 0.75 at the 150 percent start, 0.50 at 100 percent, 0.375 at the floor. Sales alone cannot sustain the pot; fee income carries it (5.9 points of volume to the engine). Sale proceeds arrive after about 50 hours when buyers pay market (statement worth about cost), so inventory of roughly 2 days of purchases sits in held statements.
- G3 do not overpay sellers: bid ceiling is `lastFill * 1.1 * (1 + 0.02 * idle 10 minute intervals)`, at most `rateCap` 3x. Windfall to the first 115 credit transaction after quiet time, market flat at P0: 1 h 0.04 eth, 2 h 0.10, 4 h 0.25, 8 h 0.66, 12 h 1.1, 24 h 1.3 eth. The 50 percent minute floor then holds the bid at 1.078x P0 after 8 h (0.5x ceiling) and 1.5x P0 after 24 h for every further credit in that minute, so volume beyond 115 credits earns 7.8 percent (8 h) to 50 percent (24 h) over market per credit. The loss is bounded by how many credits can be bought at or below market in that minute, not by the pot (190 eth pot in decision 69). A market fall leaves the bid stale because it falls only with fills (0.5 percent per credit): 30 percent fall costs about 0.09 eth, 60 percent about 0.42 eth, 90 percent about 1.2 eth. Inside the owner's goal; decision 69 quoted 4 to 157 eth at the old values.
- G4 owner pays no keeper gas unless needed: buyback is refunded from the buyback pot (at most 0.0065 eth a call). `compose` and `exitStatement` pay 0, so the owner or a holder pays: about 8.5M gas, 0.0085 eth at 1 gwei (1.2 percent of a page), 0.085 eth at 10 gwei (12 percent), 0.255 eth at 30 gwei (36 percent). Nothing in the contract rewards an outside composer, and a statement only sells after compose. FLOW 63 records the owner's "no"; the exposure is the owner's own gas when nobody composes.
- G5 `buyListing` tip is at most 4 percent of the price: 0.000356 eth for a P0 credit. At the 500,000 gas of FLOW 56 (not re-measured here) a bot breaks even only below about 0.7 gwei, so above that the owner keeper (private relay) becomes the main caller and pays gas on every success. Decision 69 names outside bots as primary.
- G6 anchor above 1: L1 above. A whale who pays the 1.5x start for a page raises the start of every later statement; the owner reset is `setAnchorBps`, an owner transaction.
- G7 `anchorMinCost` is an absolute 0.1 eth. If the credit price falls below 0.00125 eth no page moves the anchor (harmless: start stays 1.5x). If rateCap lets the price rise, the threshold shrinks relative to a page, and an attacker still controls the page cost through the pot.
