# Fixture audit f7e55fa: hidden preconditions and cold-start gaps

Scope: contracts, tests, sim and keeper at f7e55fa (origin/main 44b327a differs only under web/). Read-only audit, no contract changed.

Trigger: a launch-stalling bug that no suite caught. Fee income reaches the Core through the hook with no engine call (`Core.receive` accepts the push, `_book` runs only inside a door), while every harness reaches its state by calling a door. The unbooked window on mainnet is unbounded (no caller until the first seller, or the keeper latching the payee share), and `CoreLib.climb` clamps the climb to the booked `ethPot`.

Correction to the premise: `Fixture._fundPot` makes real hook income through the pool. `_buyCoin` and `_sellCoin` then call `core.book()` after every swap (`autoBook = true`). Only `BidRule._addToPot` (3 uses), the keeper e2e, `sim/engine.js` (`holdPot`) and `sim/market.mjs` (`POT0`) call `fund()`. Both routes run an engine door at the moment the income lands. `autoBook = false` appears at 10 sites in 6 files and every one reads state in the same block as the swap, so none of them lets time pass with unbooked income.

## Gap list (paths no suite runs from a cold start)

Class 1, state that changes with no engine call
1. Hook income on an empty pot, then time. Home: test/BidRule.t.sol. Case: `autoBook = false`, skip window, buy 1 ether, `_warp(2 hours)`, assert `ethRate()` equals the hand rate climbed from `rateStart` toward `min(ceiling, pot + pending)` and a following `sellForEth` pays the quote.
2. Drip during idle loosening and the multiplier. Home: BidRule `test_fullPotLoosensAtTheCapOverFourHours`. Case: six rounds of buy (no book) then `_warp(1 hours)`; compare `ethRate`, `climbMultiplier`, `floorRate` with `BidModel` fed pot + pending.
3. Escrow claimed by a stranger: `ESCROW.claim(address(core), address(0))` lands in `receive` as income. Home: test/V2Port.t.sol. Case: `_creditCore`, stranger claims, `collectEscrow` returns 0, the next `book` splits the claim with payee and redeem burn.
4. Forced ETH (`vm.deal(core)`) as the only income with pot 0. Home: test/FeeShare.t.sol `test_book_countsForcedEthAsIncome`. Case: warp 1 hour, read `ethRate`, sell.
5. Token donation to the balance module while the bid climbs. Home: test/BalanceBid.t.sol. Case: `xt.mint(address(mod), x)` after a warp; `balanceRate` and the next `sellForBalance` use the new `available()`.
6. Invariant harness books before every action. Home: test/invariant/HandlerBase.sol `checked` and `_afterSwap`. Case: a `buyCoinNoBook` action and a `_settle` skip flag, so a `warp` or a sell follows unbooked income; give `BidModel` a clamp from `pot + pend` that is independent of the contract.

Class 2, first-ever transitions
7. First fill from the opening rate on a hook-fed thin pot, no `setRate`, no pre-funding. Home: test/Launch.t.sol. Case: fresh fixture, skip window, one 1 ether buy, no book, warp 10 minutes, `sellForEth` of one credit; assert the pay, `lastFillTime`, anchor, peak and `spendState`.
8. First book latching the payee share called by a door instead of `book()`. Home: test/FeeShare.t.sol. Case: window over, income unbooked, `sellForEth` first; assert `payeeOn`, payee unpaid, door paid from the post-book pot.
9. First balance fill with a thin module holding. Home: BalanceBid. Case: module holds under one credit of par; payout clamps to `available()`.
10. First redeem through the real module and real Statements. Home: test/UpstreamBalance.t.sol. Case: set `REQUIRE_UPSTREAM_BALANCE=1` in ci.yml so the suite cannot skip.
11. First swapCredit at the launch fee (10,000 CC) from a coin bought in the pool. Home: test/CreditSwap.t.sol. Case: leave `swapFeeCc` at its launch value.
12. First statement sale at launch settings without the reference pin. Home: test/Sale.t.sol (new contract on `SaleBase`). Case: `anchorMinCost` 0.1 ether, drop floor 1, compose, assert the ask at ages 0, 6 and 12 hours.

Class 3, time-only evolution
13. Recovery and peak halving at launch drop (115 bps, floor 1). Home: test/Recovery.t.sol. Case: sell 80 credits, warp 6, 12 and 24 hours, compare with the model.
14. Rate after one fill with a pot that keeps growing and no further fill. Home: BidRule. Case: fill, then three hourly rounds of (buy, no book, warp); the ceiling, clamp and stored rate follow the pending pot.
15. Statement anchor decay with no buyer for 8 days at the launch `anchorMinCost`. Home: Sale `SaleAnchorTest`. Case: compose, warp 8 days, compose again, compare the two opening asks.
16. Fill after idle time without the `setRate` restatement. Home: Fixture `_fillEthPile`. Case: one suite fills at the climbed rate and checks the anchor and peak it leaves.

Class 4, keeper gating
17. Only the keeper acts, payee latched, pot 0, income pending. `decide` returns `book` only while `!payeeOn`, so `book` is skipped, and no listing is sweepable because the bid view cannot rise. Home: keeper/test/e2e/doors.e2e.ts. Case: push 0.2 ether from the hook, warp 1 hour, run a tick with `DOORS=buyListing,book`; assert a call is sent or the bid rose.
18. `bid` computed by the Core. Home: keeper/test/e2e/doors.e2e.ts. Case: take `bid` from `core.bidOf` after hook-only funding, omit `setRate`, require a sweep within a bounded number of ticks.
19. Gates acting together: listing delay 120 s, margin, base fee ceiling, `SliceTooSmall`, `NotReady`. Home: keeper/test/decide.test.ts. Case: a 24 hour tick series on a fixed state; the idle hours match an expected list with one reason per door.
20. Sim and e2e share the class 1 and 4 blind spot. Homes: sim/engine.js and script/dev/Scenario.s.sol. Cases: sim `bookEveryHours` parameter with an unbooked pending counter, and keeper delay and margin in `engineBuys`; Scenario stage `cold-fill` after `warp 2000` (one buy, no book, `warp 3600`, `sellForEth`, no `setRate`) for PHASE=pre and post.
